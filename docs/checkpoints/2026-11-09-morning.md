1. \`kernel/src/trap.S\` — added a direct-mode S-mode trap vector.
   - Allocates the 288-byte, 16-byte-aligned frame.
   - Saves x0 and x1–x31, including the interrupted stack pointer.
   - Saves \`sepc\`, \`sstatus\`, \`scause\`, and \`stval\`.
